Version 83 fixes

- Removed the unsupported Llama 4 Maverick vision fallback from the Groq backend defaults.
- Vision OCR now defaults only to the free-plan model shown in Groq Cloud: meta-llama/llama-4-scout-17b-16e-instruct.
- The Groq environment example now leaves GROQ_VISION_FALLBACK_MODEL blank so Vercel does not try a model that is unavailable on the free plan.

Recommended free-plan Vercel variables:
GROQ_MODEL=llama-3.3-70b-versatile
GROQ_FALLBACK_MODEL=openai/gpt-oss-120b
GROQ_VISION_MODEL=meta-llama/llama-4-scout-17b-16e-instruct
GROQ_VISION_FALLBACK_MODEL=
