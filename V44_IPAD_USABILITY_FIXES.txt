Dux Notes v44 iPad usability pass

Base: v43 iPad touch fixed build.

Targeted changes only:
- Added pinch zoom on the iPad writing surface using two-finger distance changes.
- Kept two-finger panning, now with pan vs pinch detection.
- Added requestAnimationFrame throttling for live ink overlay redraws.
- Added PointerEvent coalesced point support where available for smoother Apple Pencil/mouse input.
- Added handwriting smoothing when a pen/highlighter stroke is saved.
- Kept finger/palm touches guarded so they do not draw, select, drag, or trigger page copy/callout behaviour.
- Reduced top toolbar height.
- Converted main toolbar tools to icon-only buttons with hover titles and aria labels.
- Changed the secondary tool settings bar to open from the right and expand left as a compact floating strip.
- Moved Dux AI and Scratch to compact emoji-only bottom utility buttons.
- Added a Delete page action in the bottom bar and More menu.

Not changed:
- PDF import/create logic.
- PDF rendering.
- Existing notes storage.
- Flashcards.
- Study schedule.
- Dux AI backend.
- Gemini integration.
- Existing Mac desktop path.
