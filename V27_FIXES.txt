Dux Notes v27 fixes

Priority fixes:
- Reworked the toolbar so tool-specific settings now appear in a secondary drop-down settings bar below the main toolbar.
- Pen, Highlighter, Shape and Eraser controls are now easier to access without hunting through a cramped horizontal utility bar.
- More menu is now forced above the canvas with a higher z-index so the dropdown is visible.
- Ask AI button in the bottom bar has been restyled as a cleaner Dux AI pill.
- Dux AI now uses the built-in flashcard bank plus an expanded syllabus guide layer for HSC Chemistry, Physics, Maths Advanced, Extension 1, Extension 2, English Advanced, Economics and Software Engineering.

Important note:
Dux AI is still a local rule-based study helper. It does not call ChatGPT or the internet. It is much smarter than the earlier version, but not the same as a live cloud model.
